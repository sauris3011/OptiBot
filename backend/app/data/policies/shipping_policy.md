# ShopFast Shipping Policy

## Shipping speeds and cost

| Method | Delivery estimate | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 5 to 7 business days | Free on orders over $35, otherwise $4.99 |
| Express | 2 to 3 business days | $12.99 |
| Overnight | Next business day | $24.99 |

Delivery estimates are counted in business days from the date the order ships,
not the date the order is placed. Orders placed after 2:00 PM local warehouse
time begin processing on the following business day.

## Processing time

Most orders leave the warehouse within 1 business day. Orders containing
personalised or made-to-order items require 3 to 5 business days of processing
before they ship.

## Regional notes

- **West Coast (CA, OR, WA, NV, AZ):** Standard delivery typically lands at the
  fast end of the estimate, 5 business days, because these orders ship from the
  Reno fulfilment centre.
- **East Coast and Midwest:** Standard delivery typically takes the full 6 to 7
  business days.
- **Alaska and Hawaii:** Add 3 business days to every estimate. Overnight
  shipping is not available to these states.
- ShopFast does not currently ship internationally or to PO boxes.

## Carriers and tracking

ShopFast ships via FedEx, UPS, and USPS. The carrier is selected automatically
based on destination and parcel size, and cannot be chosen at checkout. A
tracking number is emailed as soon as the label is scanned, and tracking events
usually begin appearing within 6 hours of that email.

## Split shipments

Orders containing items from more than one fulfilment centre may arrive in
separate parcels on different days at no extra charge. Each parcel receives its
own tracking number.

## Delayed or lost parcels

A parcel is considered delayed once it is 3 business days past its estimated
delivery date, and lost once it is 10 business days past. Report a lost parcel
and ShopFast will either reship the order at no cost or issue a full refund.

## Address changes

A shipping address can be changed only while the order status is Processing.
Once the order ships, the address is fixed and the parcel must be returned to
sender before it can be redirected.
