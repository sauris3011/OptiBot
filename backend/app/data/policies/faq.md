# ShopFast Frequently Asked Questions

## Where is my order?

Ask the assistant with your order number and it will report the current status,
carrier, tracking number, and estimated delivery date. Order numbers are in the
format ORD-#####.

## Why does my tracking say "Label Created" but nothing else?

The label has been printed but the carrier has not yet scanned the parcel. This
normally resolves within 24 hours of the order shipping. If it persists beyond
2 business days, the parcel may have been missed at pickup and should be
reported.

## Can I cancel my order?

Orders can be cancelled without charge while the status is Processing. Once the
status changes to Shipped the order cannot be cancelled, and it must instead be
returned under the standard return policy after it arrives.

## Can I change the items in my order after placing it?

No. Items cannot be added, removed, or swapped after checkout. Cancel the order
while it is still Processing and place a new one.

## Do you price match?

ShopFast matches the advertised price of an identical in-stock item from a major
authorised retailer within 7 days of purchase. Marketplace listings, auction
sites, and clearance stock are excluded.

## How do I use a discount code?

Enter the code in the Promo Code field at checkout. Only one code may be applied
per order, and codes cannot be applied retroactively to a completed order.

## What payment methods do you accept?

Major credit and debit cards, PayPal, and ShopFast gift cards. Payment is
authorised at checkout and captured when the order ships.

## Is my order insured?

All orders are covered against loss and damage in transit at no extra cost.
Claims must be filed within 14 days of the delivery date or, for lost parcels,
within 30 days of the ship date.

## How do I contact a human agent?

Ask the assistant to escalate, or reply to any order confirmation email. Live
agents are available 7 days a week, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM Eastern.

## Do you offer gift wrapping?

Gift wrapping is available for $4.99 per order and can be selected at checkout.
Gift-wrapped orders do not include a printed invoice in the parcel.
