# ShopFast Return Policy

## Return window

ShopFast accepts returns within **14 days of delivery**. The 14-day window starts
on the date the carrier marks the order as Delivered, not the date the order was
placed. Orders that have not yet been delivered cannot be returned, but they can
be cancelled while the status is still Processing.

## Condition requirements

To qualify for a full refund, items must be:

- Unused and in their original condition
- In the original packaging with all tags attached
- Accompanied by the order number (no paper receipt is required)

Items returned outside these conditions are subject to a restocking fee of 15%
of the item price.

## Non-returnable items

The following cannot be returned under any circumstances:

- Gift cards and digital download codes
- Perishable goods
- Personal care items where the hygiene seal has been broken
- Items marked "Final Sale" on the product page

## How to start a return

1. Open the order in your ShopFast account and select **Start a return**.
2. Choose a reason and select whether you want a refund or an exchange.
3. Print the prepaid return label that is emailed to you within one hour.
4. Drop the parcel at any carrier location within 7 days of printing the label.

Return shipping is free for defective or incorrectly shipped items. For
change-of-mind returns, a flat $5.99 return shipping fee is deducted from the
refund.

## Refund timing

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Once the returned parcel is
scanned at our warehouse, refunds are processed within **3 to 5 business days**.
Your bank may take an additional 2 to 10 business days to post the credit.

## Damaged or defective items

If an item arrives damaged or defective, report it within 14 days of delivery.
Damaged-item returns are free, are not subject to a restocking fee, and are
eligible for either a replacement at no cost or a full refund including the
original shipping charge. Photographs of the damage are required before a
replacement is dispatched.
