# ShopFast Warranty Terms

## Standard coverage

All products sold by ShopFast carry a **1-year limited warranty** from the
delivery date covering manufacturing defects. Electronics carry an extended
**2-year limited warranty**. The warranty covers defects in materials and
workmanship under normal use.

## What is not covered

- Accidental damage, drops, spills, and liquid ingress
- Normal cosmetic wear such as scratches and fading
- Damage caused by unauthorised repair or modification
- Consumable parts such as batteries, filters, and belts after 90 days
- Items purchased from a seller other than ShopFast

## Filing a warranty claim

Warranty claims require the order number and a description of the fault.
Photographs or a short video of the fault are required for electronics claims.
Claims are reviewed within 2 business days.

If a claim is approved, ShopFast will at its discretion repair the item, replace
it with an equivalent model, or refund the purchase price. Replacements ship via
Express at no charge.

## Warranty versus return

The 14-day return window and the warranty are separate. A fault discovered
within 14 days of delivery should be handled as a damaged-item return, which is
faster. After 14 days, the warranty is the correct route.

## Extended protection plans

An optional ShopFast Protect plan can be purchased within 30 days of delivery.
It extends coverage to 3 years and adds accidental damage protection with a $49
service fee per claim, limited to two claims per year.
